# Research

Research is a list of one-time Insight purchases, spaced so something lands every 1–3 minutes early on and every 5 minutes late.

**Input:** the Research tab lists every item the player can see, with its cost, its effect in one line, and a time-to-afford at the current Insight rate. Tap to buy.

| Research | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salt Rakes | 50 Insight | Salt Pans ×1.5 |
| Self-Tilling Plough | 120 Insight | Farms ×2 |
| Mule Trains | 250 Insight | Porters carry ×2 |
| Hermetic Accounts | 300 Insight | Silver cap ×2 |
| Strongbox | 400 × 2^n Insight (n = Strongboxes bought) | Silver cap +500. Repeatable |
| Reed Pen of Diligent Copying | 500 Insight | Parchmenters ×2 |
| Lab Notebooks | 600 Insight | Botch chance halved |
| Apprentice Rooms | 800 Insight | +1 assistant slot per Sanctum |
| Lead-Lined Chests | 900 Insight | Vis cap +30 |
| Stones That Carry | 1,500 Insight | Marsh porters ×3 |
| Aegis of the Hearth | 2,000 Insight + 20 Vis | Hearth Notice factor 0; baseline Insight ×1.25; unlocks the Drowned Gate |
| Marsh Mist | 2,500 Insight + 10 Vis | All Notice generation ×0.6 |

- The list has room for 3–4 more items between 1,000 and 2,500 Insight. They're written as content and tuned with the balance simulation.
- **Feedback:** buying an item plays a bell, flashes what it affects, and adds a line to the Chronicle tab.
- **Why:** each item changes what the player does next (Kittens Game's techs, Factorio's research). The Aegis is the one purchase every full run must make, because it opens the path to victory. Strongbox gives Silver somewhere to grow as Endowments get more expensive.
